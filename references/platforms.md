# Platform Guidance

Use this reference when creating or reviewing content for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, or X.

Do not duplicate one text across platforms. Preserve the core message while changing the format, pacing, depth, opening, and call to action.

## Instagram

Use Instagram for visual storytelling, saves, shares, and community interaction.

### Suitable formats

- Carousel
- Reel
- Single-image post
- Story adaptation

### Instagram post

- Begin with a clear first line that creates interest without misleading.
- Keep paragraphs short and easy to scan.
- Build one main idea rather than several competing messages.
- Use a call to action suited to the objective, such as saving, sharing, commenting, visiting, or requesting information.
- Use a limited number of relevant hashtags only when they add discovery value.

### Carousel

Provide:

1. Cover text
2. Slide-by-slide structure
3. One clear point per slide
4. A logical progression
5. A final takeaway or call to action

Keep cover text concise and visually readable.

### Reel

Provide:

- Hook for the first three seconds
- Spoken script
- Scene or shot sequence
- On-screen text
- Suggested visual rhythm
- Ending and call to action

Do not overload the video with unnecessary transitions or text.

## TikTok

Use TikTok for fast, human, conversational, and visually active content.

### TikTok script

- Start directly with the problem, question, result, or surprising observation.
- Avoid long introductions and formal greetings.
- Use natural spoken language.
- Maintain momentum through clear beats or scene changes.
- Demonstrate, explain, compare, or tell a short story.
- End with a natural invitation to respond, follow, try, or continue to another part.

Provide:

- Target duration
- Opening hook
- Spoken script
- Scene breakdown
- On-screen text
- Suggested shots
- Closing action

Do not invent a trend, sound, challenge, or platform feature. If live trend information is unavailable, label any trend-based idea as requiring current verification.

## LinkedIn

Use LinkedIn for professional insight, expertise, lessons, case-based content, and constructive discussion.

### LinkedIn post

- Open with a meaningful professional observation, tension, lesson, or question.
- Establish why the subject matters.
- Develop one coherent argument or experience.
- Use examples only when supplied or clearly presented as hypothetical.
- Keep the tone credible and human rather than promotional or overly formal.
- End with a useful takeaway or a focused discussion question.

For personal-brand stories:

- Preserve the user's real experience.
- Do not invent achievements, employers, results, or personal details.
- Connect the experience to a lesson useful to the audience.

For business content:

- Explain value before promoting the product or service.
- Avoid unsupported claims and exaggerated leadership language.

## X

Use X for concise insight, timely discussion, clear opinions, and structured threads.

### Single post

- Express one main idea.
- Remove unnecessary introductions.
- Make the wording precise and easy to understand.
- Use a question only when it invites meaningful discussion.

### Thread

Provide:

1. A strong opening post that states the subject and value.
2. A logical sequence in which each post adds new information.
3. Short, focused sections.
4. A final summary, takeaway, or question.

Do not divide a long paragraph arbitrarily. Each post should have an individual function within the thread.

## Cross-Platform Campaign Design

Assign a distinct role to each selected platform.

Example:

- TikTok or Reel introduces the problem through a short video.
- Instagram carousel explains the practical steps.
- LinkedIn provides professional context or a lesson.
- X opens a focused discussion or summarizes key observations.

Change the content architecture rather than only changing its length.

## Platform Selection

When the user has not selected platforms:

- Recommend only the platforms that serve the audience, objective, and available production resources.
- Explain the recommendation briefly.
- Do not require the user to publish on every supported platform.

## Timing, Trends, and Hashtags

Publishing timing, current trends, and hashtag performance can change.

- Do not present a publishing time as universally optimal.
- Do not invent current trend data.
- Do not promise reach based on hashtags.
- Label timing and trend suggestions as hypotheses that should be tested or verified using current account data.
